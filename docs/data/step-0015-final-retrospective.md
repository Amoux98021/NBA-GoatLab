## Permanent limits and reopening criteria

GOATLab cannot eliminate unequal historical measurement across NBA eras. Its method attempts to
represent that limitation through calibrated uncertainty instead of fabricated precision. Wider
uncertainty does not imply lower basketball quality. Presence is observational, some evidence
regimes remain interval-only, and shared global calibration-model uncertainty is not fully
modeled. Active players are evaluated only through the 2025-26 cutoff.

Future work must not reopen basketball formulas because a leaderboard looks unconventional; tune
around Jordan, LeBron, or any famous player; penalize uncertainty; make missing values zero;
renormalize a required dimension away; treat interval centers as exact points; or describe a
navigation rank as certain superiority. A new concrete implementation/methodological defect or
material factual data source is required for a new research methodology step.

STEP-0015 is closed. STEP-0016 begins productization of the frozen probabilistic contract; it
does not authorize a basketball formula change.
