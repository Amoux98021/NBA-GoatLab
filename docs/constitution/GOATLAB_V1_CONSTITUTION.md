# GOATLab V1 Constitution

Status: Binding for V1 dimension methodology as of STEP-0014.

This machine-facing Constitution distills the binding rules in the audited human-facing `GOATLab V1 Constitution & Methodology Notes` Draft 0.8. The longer document remains the research history and paper backbone. If an earlier experimental candidate conflicts with this document, this Constitution governs V1.

## Architecture and evidence

- Silver contains facts. Gold contains derived analytical features. Ranking and scoring code contains normative choices.
- Historical absence is not performance. `NULL`, `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE`, `NOT_APPLICABLE`, insufficient sample, and observed zero remain distinct.
- Missing evidence may lower confidence but must not directly lower player quality.
- Era normalization is always active infrastructure, not an optional preference.
- Modern-only evidence is enrichment unless a documented restricted-feature overlap bridge demonstrates comparable all-era meaning.
- Active careers are accomplishments through the frozen corpus cutoff only. No projections or completion penalties are allowed.
- No external ranking is an optimization target. No player-specific correction is permitted.
- Era Dominance is diagnostic and is not an eighth additive dimension.
- Every formula, source lineage, coverage rule, random seed, output, and fingerprint is versioned and reproducible.

## Evidence ownership

- Peak owns the height of regular-season overall performance.
- Longevity owns the duration and persistence of high-level regular-season performance.
- Offense owns regular-season offensive value creation.
- Defense owns regular-season defensive value prevention.
- Playoffs owns individual postseason performance, including postseason offense and defense.
- Accolades owns formal recognition.
- Winning owns team outcomes and sustained team success conditioned on meaningful participation.
- One fact may inform two dimensions only when it answers two genuinely different questions. It may not receive duplicate credit for the same meaning.

## Peak

- The best complete contiguous three-season peak is primary.
- The best single-season apex is secondary.
- The three-season weight must exceed the single-season weight.
- Five-year coverage is not a required V1 anchor.
- Peak excludes awards, championships, team outcomes, long-career accumulation, and an extra Era Dominance vote.

## Longevity

- Longevity means sustained greatness, not seasons employed, games alone, or an arithmetic average of every season.
- Below-threshold seasons add little or no longevity credit and do not erase elite seasons.
- V1 combines elite-season breadth, capped cumulative high-level value, and consecutive persistence.
- Per-season contribution is thresholded and capped so extreme apex height is owned by Peak.
- All-NBA is calibration evidence only. It is not a Longevity input.

## Offense

- V1 uses Global Value Creation.
- Scoring volume and era-relative efficiency first form one joint Scoring Value axis; they are not independent top-level votes.
- Creation and playmaking form a second primary pathway. Assists are evidence, not a complete definition of creation.
- The combination must allow an elite primary pathway plus meaningful secondary-pathway credit without requiring equal skill-box strength.
- Postseason offense belongs to Playoffs.
- Tracking, gravity, off-ball, possession, and advanced evidence remain enrichment unless bridged.

## Defense

- V1 uses Triangulated Defensive Impact: team/opponent suppression and player-team impact form the core; individual actions add evidence where reliable; role provides interpretation.
- Defense is not steals plus blocks, awards, or team defense alone.
- Position context must not force equal defensive ceilings.
- DPOY and All-Defense are Accolades inputs and Defense validation/calibration evidence only; they are not additive Defense points.
- Postseason defense belongs to Playoffs.
- Every Defense score carries separate evidence confidence. Missing historical defensive statistics never become zero.
- Advanced/tracking defense does not create a modern bonus without a passing bridge.

## Playoffs

- Absolute individual postseason excellence is primary and must outweigh all secondary evidence combined.
- Regular-season-to-playoff resilience/lift is bounded secondary evidence.
- Repeated elite postseason performance is supporting evidence.
- Team advancement and championships are not individual Playoffs points.
- Small samples lower confidence, not the quality score.
- Opponent difficulty may contextualize resilience but cannot become disguised series-win credit.

## Accolades

- V1 uses two axes: Major Honors and Sustained Recognition.
- Major Honors have stronger per-event influence; sustained All-NBA, All-Defense, and All-Star recognition remains substantial.
- Same-season related events are bundled/capped rather than fully stacked.
- Statistical titles are bounded supporting evidence because their production is already represented elsewhere.
- NBA Champion events belong to Winning, not Accolades.
- Specialized/recent awards do not receive universal core credit merely because they exist.
- Unanimous MVP status remains a factual annotation, not a large binary bonus. Any future voting-strength input must be continuous and regime-aware.

## Winning

- V1 uses Balanced Contextual Winning: observed outcomes are primary; sustained elite team success is major; participation conditions credit.
- Each player-team-season receives one highest postseason outcome tier. Championship, Finals, and earlier-round credit cannot fully stack within the same season.
- Sustained Winning accumulates outcomes across different seasons.
- Regular membership, playoff participation, Finals participation, and role/opportunity evidence remain distinct.
- Path difficulty is a bounded contextual modifier and cannot outweigh the outcome.
- Generic WOWY/team lift is a diagnostic attribution aid in V1, not an additive Winning component. Defense-specific suppression may belong in Defense.

## Scores, confidence, and future Overall eligibility

- Each dimension uses a deterministic common 0--100 interpretation that is resistant to raw outlier compression.
- Coverage and confidence are reported separately and never multiplied into quality.
- Available-evidence renormalization is permitted only when formula-specific, explicit, and reported; it may never be silent.
- A genuinely unavailable required dimension makes a future official Overall result provisional/unranked. Its weight may not be redistributed across other dimensions.
- User-created rankings may omit a dimension only when the user explicitly assigns it zero weight.
- No official Overall dimension weights are selected in STEP-0014.

## Required constitutional tests

- Peak versus Longevity: capped longevity contribution prevents apex replication.
- Regular season versus playoffs: Offense and Defense exclude postseason performance.
- Playoffs versus Winning: performance and outcome use separate evidence.
- Winning: one mutually exclusive highest outcome tier per season.
- Defense versus Accolades: DPOY and All-Defense never add Defense points.
- Offense versus Accolades: statistical titles never recreate scoring performance.
- Accolades: related same-season events are bundled and capped.
- WOWY: generic lift is not duplicated across Winning, Offense, and Defense.
- Era fairness: modern-only features cannot directly increase the universal score without a passing bridge.
- Missingness: unavailable states never become zero.
- Active careers: cutoff accomplishments only, with no forecast.

## Overall methodology boundary

STEP-0014 may audit correlations, PCA/factors, residual variance, and random-simplex overall-weight sensitivity. Those diagnostics are not an official GOAT ranking and do not select default Overall weights. STEP-0015 must use the finalized seven scores to audit effective influence and choose any default Overall methodology.

## STEP-0015K measurement-policy clarification

This section clarifies how the existing philosophy is implemented after the STEP-0015 forensic
audits; it does not change the definitions of Peak or Longevity.

- Peak uses uncertainty-aware tiered player-season measurement. Its constitutional formula remains
  70% best contiguous three-year quality plus 30% single-season apex. The best window and apex are
  reselected inside correlated career simulations, and point availability depends on validated
  aggregate calibration.
- Longevity retains 35% elite-season breadth at P80, 40% capped P80-P90 area, and 25% longest P80
  run. When evidence cannot support comparable rank-grade precision, Longevity is interval-only.
- A range is not a lower score. Confidence and uncertainty describe evidence precision and never
  penalize quality.
- An interval midpoint is not an official dimension point. An interval-only or unavailable
  required dimension may not be dropped or cause silent Overall weight renormalization.
- `goatlab-v1-overall-v1` remains the frozen historical baseline until a distinct
  uncertainty-aware Overall methodology is validated and explicitly versioned.
- Active careers remain `TO_DATE_NO_PROJECTION` through the frozen 2025-26 cutoff.

## STEP-0015M Defense measurement-policy clarification

This section implements Triangulated Defensive Impact after the V1 attribution audit; it does not
change Defense's philosophical definition.

- `goatlab-v1-defense-v2-tiered` bounds era-relative Team Context at 10% of the rich reference
  form. Individual role-aware actions and observed Presence are primary.
- Role-aware actions remain 40% rebound evidence, 30% steals, and 30% blocks. Broad-role context
  interprets action evidence and never forces equal defensive ceilings.
- Defensive Presence Impact is observational, reliability-weighted Regular Season evidence. It is
  neither RAPM nor a causal estimate.
- Missing Presence is never predicted, zero-filled, or silently reallocated into team/actions.
  Weaker factual forms are linked to the rich-form scale and receive calibrated uncertainty.
- Defense status is `OFFICIAL_DEFENSE_POINT`, `PROVISIONAL_DEFENSE_POINT`,
  `DEFENSE_INTERVAL_ONLY`, or `DEFENSE_UNAVAILABLE`. An interval center is not an official point.
- DPOY, All-Defense, postseason evidence, championships, and modern-only metrics do not add Defense
  points. Awards and richer modern evidence remain validation only.
- Defense uncertainty may depend on Peak/Longevity uncertainty because the dimensions share
  evidence. A future Overall simulation may not assume those errors are independent.
- Defense V1 and Overall V1 remain permanent reproducible historical baselines.

## STEP-0015N Overall promotion-policy clarification

This section records the result of the final joint uncertainty promotion audit; it does not alter
the seven frozen Overall weights or create a successor ranking.

- Peak/Longevity paired uncertainty paths must remain paired. Defense uncertainty must be coupled
  through a validated marginal-preserving dependence architecture rather than assumed independent.
- A joint evidence pattern may receive an Overall point only when its complete Overall distribution
  passes the frozen aggregate calibration gates. A dimension's interval center is never promoted
  to an exact point merely because it enters a weighted simulation.
- A deterministic ranking population must not be structurally selected by evidence regime. A
  ranking that excludes most otherwise plausible candidates because their calibrated Overall is
  interval-only is not eligible for publication.
- STEP-0015N validates the research propagation architecture but does not promote it: only 17 of
  the archived V1 Top 100 receive ranking-grade V2 points, while 83 remain interval-only.
- `goatlab-v1-overall-v1` and its archived Top 100 therefore remain the official frozen baseline.
  No Overall V2 Top 100 exists. Future work must resolve ranking/publication semantics without
  lowering calibration gates, renormalizing weights, or treating intervals as exact points.

## STEP-0015P probabilistic-first publication governance

This subsequent governance decision supplements the historical STEP-0015N finding; it does not
retroactively promote Overall V2 as an exact-point method or rewrite its failed point gate.

- The canonical comparison object is a calibrated Overall probability distribution and its derived
  rank distribution. An exact ordinal position is not scientific truth.
- `goatlab-v1-ranking-policy-v2-probabilistic` permits future navigation by median-rank sort,
  with canonical-player-ID tie breaking, if rank bands, Top-N probabilities, Overall ranges,
  evidence statuses, cutoff, and conditional-measurement caveats remain visible. A display `#N`
  is a navigational summary only. STEP-0015P creates no new Top-100 artifact.
- An official, provisional, or interval-native player is distribution-rankable only when the
  calibrated seven-dimensional Overall distribution is usable. Exact-point status is not required.
  Unavailable required evidence means unranked/insufficient evidence, not zero quality.
- A diagnostic interval center alone is not an official point. Confidence and interval width never
  penalize quality. Weights are not silently redistributed around missing dimensions.
- Publish pairwise uncertainty, not an unsupported close-order claim. Top-100 membership and
  pairwise labels describe probabilities and cannot modify a quality score.
- All probabilities are conditional on the frozen measurement architecture. Shared cross-player
  calibration-model uncertainty is not fully represented. Active careers remain
  `TO_DATE_NO_PROJECTION` through the 2025-26 cutoff.
- GOATLab cannot eliminate unequal historical measurement environments. It reports calibrated
  uncertainty rather than fabricated precision. A wider interval is not a lower player rating.
- The STEP-0015 forensic branch is closed. Reopening a basketball methodology requires a concrete
  defect or material new factual evidence, not aesthetically unexpected player placements.
